# Example: System Design Interview Practice

## Prompt

```
/design Let's do a system design interview — design Twitter
```

## What happens

The orchestrator runs a collaborative interview-style session:

- Asks YOU to drive the clarification (like a real interview)
- Nudges if you miss key constraints
- Presents trade-offs for you to evaluate
- Throws curveballs: "Your celebrity user just tweeted to 50M followers — walk me through what happens"
- Gives feedback on your reasoning

## Tips for interview prep

1. **Start with requirements** — Don't jump to architecture
2. **Do the math** — Interviewers love back-of-the-envelope estimates
3. **Name your trade-offs** — "I'm choosing X because Y, accepting Z as a downside"
4. **Think about failure** — "If this node goes down, here's what happens"
5. **Iterate** — Change a constraint and re-evaluate: "What if we need 10x the throughput?"
